About

I am a Research and Innovation Engineer at the University of Cyprus, based at the PHAETHON Centre of Excellence, specifically the Innovation team. I work on designing, making, and coding prototype devices to be used in research, lately with a focus in the photovoltaics space. I am completing my PhD at University College London, where I worked on do-it-yourself assistive technology (DIY-AT) by designing various devices. Before that, I studied Biomedical Engineering (MEng) at Imperial College London.

Current interests: digital fabrication, electronics development, open hardware research, and home automation.
