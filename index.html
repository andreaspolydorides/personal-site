About

I am a Research and Innovation Engineer at the University of Cyprus, based at the PHAETHON Centre of Excellence. I work on designing, making, and coding prototype devices to be used in research.
I am completing my PhD at University College London, where I focused on the interaction of wheelchair users with do-it-yourself assistive technology (DIY-AT). Before that, I graduated from Imperial College London with a degree in Biomedical Engineering (MEng).

Current interests: digital fabrication, electronics development, open hardware research, and home automation.
